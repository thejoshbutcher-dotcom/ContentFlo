How it works

One flow. Start to finish.

Research→ Idea→ Script→ Record→ Edit→ Review→ Publish
STEP 1

Set up your brand & research

Add your niche, your audience, the competitor channels you study, and the videos that inspire you. CreatorFlo flags what's outperforming and keeps it all in one swipe file — so you always have a steady flow of ideas worth making.

STEP 2

Recreate a winner & script it

Spot an outlier, hit Recreate, and it lands in your pipeline with the original pinned as its reference. Open the card to a ready-made script template with hooks, frameworks, and CTAs for that exact format.

STEP 3

Drag it to done

Move cards through scripting, filming, editing and review — solo or with your team — then drop them on the calendar. Open the app any morning and know exactly what today is for.
